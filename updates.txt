Final Level: Lava Tunnel Exploration
In this final level, Laika enters a lava tunnel, which provides shelter and protects her batteries from the harsh environmental conditions and high radiation on Mars.
Inside the tunnel, the player must find and interact with two buttons: one representing a rock sample and another representing a camera. Once both objectives are completed, Laika can leave the tunnel and head toward the final destination point.
Reaching the final goal will complete the mission and allow Laika to return home to Earth.
